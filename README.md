Analyzed a network of 3,000+ Facebook TV show pages to understand how entertainment-related pages are connected and identify influential pages and communities within the network. From a business perspective, the analysis could support social media, marketing, or media strategy teams seeking to understand audience ecosystems and relationships between entertainment brands. I calculated network characteristics including nodes, edges, connected components, diameter, and centrality measures, and applied community detection techniques to identify clusters of closely connected TV show pages. The resulting network visualizations and metrics help stakeholders identify influential pages, understand how content communities are structured, and make more informed decisions about audience targeting, cross-promotion, partnerships, and marketing outreach. The project demonstrates how social network analysis can transform relationship data into actionable insights about influence and community structure.
